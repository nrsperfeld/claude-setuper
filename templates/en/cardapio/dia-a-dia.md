Claude menu

Ready-made requests you can copy, paste or say out loud (hold the space bar). Swap whatever is in brackets for your own situation. You don't have to phrase it perfectly: a loose sentence works fine.

Home and routine
- Organize my Downloads folder into folders by file type, without deleting anything.
- Make this week's shopping list for [how many people], using what I tell you we're out of.
- Make a simple meal plan for the next 5 days, using what I have at home.
- Remind me what I need to pay this month: I'll dictate the bills and you make a table with the due dates.

Paperwork and documents
- [drag in a photo or PDF] Explain this document to me in five lines and tell me if there's anything I need to do.
- [drag in a screenshot] Turn this screenshot into organized text.
- Read this contract and tell me what could hurt me, in plain English.

Messages
- Write a polite but firm message to [person] about [subject].
- Improve this text I'm about to paste, without changing the way I talk.
- Help me answer this email: [paste the email].

Planning
- Plan a day trip to [place] with times, costs and a backup plan in case it rains.
- Compare these three options I'm about to paste and tell me which one is the better choice and why.
- Help me decide [decision]: ask me the right questions before giving your opinion.

Learning
- Explain [subject] to me like I'm 12, with an everyday example.
- Teach me how to do [something on the computer] step by step, waiting for me to finish each step.

Memory
- Note in my second brain that I prefer [way of doing things].
- What did we decide about [subject]?
