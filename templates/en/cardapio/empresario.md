Claude menu

Ready-made requests you can copy, paste or say out loud (hold the space bar). Swap whatever is in brackets for your own situation.

Numbers
- [drag in the spreadsheet] Show me the 3 most important things in these numbers and what you would do.
- Run the numbers: if I [raise prices / hire someone / cut a cost], how much does it cost, how much comes back and how long does it take?
- Make a simple cash flow spreadsheet for the next 3 months from what I tell you.
- Where am I losing money? I'll paste this month's costs.

Clients and sales
- Put together a proposal for [client] with this information: [what they need, deadline, price].
- Read these messages from the client and tell me what they really want and how to answer.
- Write a polite, firm payment reminder for someone who is [days] late.
- Give me 5 questions to ask in my next sales meeting with [type of client].

Team
- Write the procedure for [task] so the team can follow it without asking me.
- Write the message to the team about [change], firm on results and respectful of people.
- Put together Monday's meeting agenda with what needs to be decided on each point.
- Help me prepare for a hard conversation with [person] about [subject].

Decisions
- Help me decide between [option A] and [option B]: ask your questions before giving your opinion.
- Summarize this meeting [paste the notes or transcript] with decisions, owners and deadlines.

Memory
- Note in my second brain the decision we made about [subject] and why.
- What did we decide about [subject] last month?
