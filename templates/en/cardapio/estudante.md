Claude menu

Ready-made requests you can copy, paste or say out loud (hold the space bar). Swap whatever is in brackets for your own situation. The AI is set up to help you learn, so it will make you think before it gives you the answer.

Studying
- Make a study plan for the [subject] test on [date] that fits my routine.
- Ask me 5 questions about [topic], one at a time, without giving the answer before I try.
- Explain [hard material] with an example from my daily life, and then I'll explain it back to you.
- Turn my notes [paste or drag in] into a summary and review questions.

Assignments and essays
- Review my essay and point out the pattern in my mistakes, without rewriting it for me.
- Help me outline a paper on [topic]: what to research and in what order.
- Is this argument any good? Show me the weak spot.

Memorizing
- Come up with a way to remember [list of formulas, dates, words].
- Give me a quick 10-question quiz on [topic] and tell me where I make the most mistakes.

Getting organized
- Make my schedule for the week with classes, studying and rest.
- Organize my [subject] folder on my computer, without deleting anything.

Patterns (what helps you learn faster)
- What pattern shows up in my mistakes in [subject]?
- Note in my second brain the way of studying that worked for me.
- What have I already learned about [topic]?
