This screen is Maestri. The big window on the left is your AI. Its name is Claude. You talk to it by typing down there and pressing Enter, just like texting.

You can write however you normally talk, no special formula needed. It understands loose sentences, half-finished questions and confusing requests. If it doesn't understand, it asks.

These colored notes are reminders. You can edit, move or delete any of them. To make a new note, click the note icon at the top and draw a rectangle on the background.

To see more or less of the screen, use two fingers on the trackpad (pinch to zoom, drag to move).
