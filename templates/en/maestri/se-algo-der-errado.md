If something goes wrong

- The AI window closed or froze: click it and press Enter. If it doesn't come back, right-click it and choose restart.
- It stopped answering partway through: press Esc once and repeat your request.
- To check that the installation is fine: open Terminal and type `claude-setuper doctor`.
- There's an installation report at: Home folder → "Claude Setuper" → report.txt. Send it to whoever helped you install.
