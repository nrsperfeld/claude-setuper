AI basics

Talking instead of typing
- Voice mode is already on. Click the AI window, hold the space bar, talk, and let go. What you said shows up as text. Check it and press Enter to send.
- The first time, your Mac asks if Maestri can use the microphone. Click Allow.
- If the space bar just types a space instead of recording, type /voice and press Enter. That turns voice mode back on.

Chatting
- Enter sends the message.
- To show it a file or a screenshot, drag it into the AI window.
- Pressing Esc once makes it stop what it's doing. Pressing Esc twice takes you back to your previous message, so you can fix it and send it again.
- When it asks for permission, numbered options appear. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or press the number.

Starting fresh or going back to a conversation
- /clear starts a new, clean conversation. What it wrote down in your second brain stays saved.
- /resume shows your earlier conversations so you can pick up where you left off.

More than one AI at a time
- Command + T opens a new terminal here in Maestri. If it asks which agent, choose Claude Code. If it only opens a black screen, type claude and press Enter.
- Each window is a separate AI, each doing its own work. You can have one writing an email while another organizes a spreadsheet.
- Command + Shift + D makes a new note like this one.

Help
- /help shows all the commands.
- When in doubt, ask the AI itself: "how do I...?". It will explain.
