What you can ask the AI

- "Help me write an email to a client who paid late"
- "Organize these ideas I'm about to paste here"
- "Read this file and summarize it in five lines" (drag the file into the window)
- "Make a table with my costs for the month"
- "Explain this to me like I'm 12"
- "Build a web page to promote my service"
- "What did we decide about project X?" (it searches your second brain)

The more context you give about your work, the better it gets. And it keeps what it learns.
