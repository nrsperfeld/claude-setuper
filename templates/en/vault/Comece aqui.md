Hi, {{nome}}. This is your second brain. It's a folder of notes on your computer, and Obsidian is just the app that shows those notes in a nice way.

The important part: the AI reads this second brain before working with you and writes in it when it finishes. So, over time, it remembers how you like things, what has already been decided and where each project stopped. You don't have to do anything to make this happen.

Where things are:

- `CLAUDE/` is the AI's folder. Claude is the name of your AI, which is why the folder is called that. It writes there what it learns about you: corrections, decisions, patterns, ideas and the state of each project. Feel free to read it, and to edit or delete whatever you want. You're the one in charge.
- `CLAUDE/wiki/` brings together, in one page per subject, what the notes say about each person, project or topic. The page [[How to work with me]] gathers the rules the AI learned from your corrections, and it reads that page at the start of every conversation.
- `CLAUDE/inbox/` is the inbox folder. Drag in a PDF, a text or a screenshot you want to keep, and the AI reads it once a day. If you want it right away, ask the AI to /save it.

The folders below are yours. The AI reads them but doesn't write in them.

- `1. PROJECTS/` is for things with a beginning, a middle and an end.
- `2. AREAS/` is for things that never finish: health, family, your business.
- `3. RESOURCES/` is for reference material: books, courses, references.
- `4. ARCHIVE/` is where things go when they're no longer active.

Three things you can do now:

1. Open the note [[Claude dashboard]]. It automatically shows the latest things the AI has written down.
2. Create a note in `2. AREAS/` about your work, with whatever you'd like to share. The AI will read it.
3. Talk to the AI {{comoAbrir}} and ask: "save a decision: ...". Then come back here and watch the note appear in `CLAUDE/decisions/`.
