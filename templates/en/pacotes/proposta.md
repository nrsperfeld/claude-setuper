name: proposal
description: Puts together a sales proposal or quote. Use with "write a proposal for", "put together a quote", "/proposal".
---

Find out from the person, one question at a time, only what isn't clear: who the client is, what their problem is, what will be delivered, for how much, how long it will take, and what happens if they say yes (the next step).

Write the proposal in plain text with these parts, in this order: what the client wants to solve (in their words), what will be done, what the client receives, timeline, price, payment terms, how long the proposal is valid and the next step. One page, nothing decorative.

Deliver it in Markdown ready to copy and offer to generate a PDF if the person wants one. Note the client and the amount in `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/projects/<client name>.md` (no heading, ending with `#claude #project`).
