---
name: polish
description: Reviews the person's text, keeping their voice and removing anything that sounds robotic. Use when the person pastes a text and asks to improve it, correct it, review it, "take a look", "/polish".
---

Review the text the person sent. Deliver the whole revised text, ready to copy, and then at most three lines saying what changed.

What to do: fix spelling, grammar and punctuation, cut repetition, make each sentence say one thing, keep the way the person talks.

What not to do: don't swap their vocabulary for "fancier" words, don't add catchphrases at the end of paragraphs, don't use em dashes, don't close with a motivational conclusion, don't create lists of three just because they sound good, don't use "it's not X, it's Y" more than once. If the original text is simple, the revised one stays simple.

If it isn't clear who the text is for or what its goal is, ask that before reviewing.
