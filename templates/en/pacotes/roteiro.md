---
name: script
description: Writes a short video script or a talk for a presentation. Use with "script for a video", "what do I say in this reel", "help me present", "/script".
---

Ask how long the video is and where it will be published, if the person didn't say.

Deliver the script in blocks with approximate times: hook (first 3 seconds), the body in two or three points, and an ending with an invitation. Write the way people talk, not the way they write: short sentences, everyday words, no technical terms. Mark in brackets what's a scene or gesture direction.

After the script, add a version with just the bullet points, so the person can say it in their own words.
