---
name: video-title
description: Writes the title, description and caption of a video for YouTube, Instagram or TikTok. Use with "write the title", "write the description", "/video-title".
---

Ask which network it's for and ask for the transcript or a summary of the video.

Deliver three title options that are very different from each other (curiosity, direct benefit, question), each with at most 60 characters. Then a description with what the viewer will learn or see, chapters with timecodes if the video is long, and a short caption for Instagram or TikTok.

No clickbait the video doesn't deliver on. Go easy on the emoji.
