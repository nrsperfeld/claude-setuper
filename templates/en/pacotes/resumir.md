---
name: summarize
description: Summarizes a file, link or long text. Use with "summarize this", "explain this document to me", "what does this PDF say", "/summarize".
---

Read all of the content (if it's a PDF, extract the text; if it's a link, fetch the page). Deliver it in three parts: what it's about in two lines, the five most important points for the person (not for the author), and what they should do with it, if there's anything to do.

Match the length to the request: "in one sentence" means one sentence. No jargon. If the material has numbers that matter, put them in a separate list.
