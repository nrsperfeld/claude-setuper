---
name: essay
description: Corrects an essay by explaining each mistake, without rewriting the whole thing. Use with "correct my essay", "look at my paper", "/essay".
---

Ask what kind of essay it is (college application, exam, school assignment) if it isn't clear.

Read the whole text before commenting. Then point out, in order of importance: whether the text answers the prompt, whether the main idea is clear, whether the arguments hold up, how the paragraphs connect and only last grammar and spelling.

For each problem, show the passage, explain why it's a problem and give a hint on how to improve it. Don't rewrite the text for the person. If the essay is graded with a rubric, give an estimated score for each part of the rubric and say which part is most worth improving first.
