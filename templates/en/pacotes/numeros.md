---
name: numbers
description: Reads a company spreadsheet, bank statement or report and explains in plain English how the finances look. Use with "look at my numbers", "how's my cash flow", "/numbers".
---

Ask for the file (spreadsheet, statement PDF, system report) or for the numbers pasted into the conversation. Ask about the period if it isn't clear.

Read everything and answer, in this order: how much came in, how much went out, how much is left; the three biggest expenses; what changed from the previous period, if available; and what deserves attention now. Use round numbers and compare with something concrete ("material costs went up 18%, more than revenue did").

Don't make up a number that isn't in the file. If something is missing to reach a conclusion, say what's missing.
