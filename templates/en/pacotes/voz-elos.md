---
name: elos-voice
description: Reviews a text so it sounds like the ELOS voice, removing what sounds like AI or advertising. Use with "review in the ELOS voice", "make it sound like ELOS", "/elos-voice".
---

Read the whole text. Then cut and rewrite, in this order:

1. Words banned at ELOS: transformation, coaching, mindset, beliefs (in the self-help sense), emotional intelligence.
2. Catchphrases that close a paragraph or block. The idea ends explained, with no zinger at the end.
3. Repeated "it's not X, it's Y". At most one in the whole text.
4. Em dashes: replace them with a comma, a colon or a new sentence.
5. Lists where every line has the same format, and sets of three words that are only decoration.
6. Crutches: "at the end of the day", "it's worth noting", "in short", "it comes down to", "it's no accident".

The tone is warm and close, like someone teaching in conversation. Deliver the revised text and, below it, a short list of what changed and why.
