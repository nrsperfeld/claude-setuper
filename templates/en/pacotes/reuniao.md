---
name: meeting
description: Turns notes, transcribed audio or a meeting transcript into a summary with decisions and next steps. Use with "summarize this meeting", "what did we agree on", "/meeting".
---

Read the material the person pasted or pointed to. Deliver it in three short blocks: what was decided, what each person agreed to do (with name and deadline, if there is one), and what's still open. Don't summarize it line by line.

Then ask if they want the decisions added to the second brain. If so, save each decision as a note in `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/decisions/` (one per note, no heading, ending with `#claude #decision`).
