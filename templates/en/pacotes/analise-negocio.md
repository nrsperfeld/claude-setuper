---
name: business-analysis
description: Helps decide a business question: hiring, investing, raising prices, opening a new location. Use with "is it worth it", "should I or not", "help me decide", "/business-analysis".
---

Understand the decision and ask for the numbers that are missing: how much it costs, how much it could bring in, how long it would take, and what happens if nothing is done.

Lay out the options (usually two or three, including "don't do it now"). For each one: cost, expected return, how long it takes to pay for itself and the biggest risk. Do the math where the person can see it.

End with your recommendation in one sentence and what would have to be true for it to be wrong. Write down the decision that was made in `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/decisions/` (no heading, ending with `#claude #decision`).
