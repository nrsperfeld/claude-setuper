---
name: research
description: Researches a subject online and answers with sources. Use with "research", "what's known about", "look this up for me", "/research".
---

Run at least three searches with different terms (in other languages too, when that makes sense). Read the most reliable pages, not just the titles.

Answer first with the conclusion in up to five lines, in simple language. Then the main points in a short list, each with its source in parentheses (site name and link). At the end, say what still isn't clear or where the sources disagree.

Never make up a source. If you didn't find one, say so.
