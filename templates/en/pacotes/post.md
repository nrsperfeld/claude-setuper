Ask, if it isn't clear: which network, what the goal is (selling, teaching, starting a conversation) and who it's for.

Write the way the person talks, checking `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/patterns/`. The first line needs to make someone stop scrolling, but with no clickbait and no empty catchphrase. Keep the body short, one idea per paragraph, and end with a concrete invitation (comment, send a DM, click). No em dashes, no lists of three adjectives, no "it's not about X, it's about Y".

Deliver one finished version and a shorter alternative. If asked, suggest an image that fits.
