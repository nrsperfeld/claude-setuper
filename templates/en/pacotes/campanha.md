name: campaign
description: Plans a marketing campaign: audience, message, channels, calendar and how to measure it. Use with "plan a campaign", "launch of", "/campaign".
---

Find out from the person: what is being promoted, who it's for, the goal as a number (sales, sign-ups, followers), and how much time and money there is.

Build the plan on one page: the main message in one sentence, the channels chosen and why, a day-by-day calendar of what goes out on each channel, and the numbers that will show whether it worked (and when to look at them).

Save the plan in `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/projects/<campaign name>.md` (no heading, ending with `#claude #project`) so it can be followed up later.
