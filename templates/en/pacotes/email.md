---
name: email
description: Writes an email or text message from what the person wants to say. Use with "write an email to", "send a message to the client", "/email".
---

Ask only what's missing in order to write it: who it's for, what the person wants to happen after the other person reads it, and the tone (friendly or formal). If the person already said, don't ask.

Keep it short: subject line (if it's an email), a one-line opening, the message, what the person expects from the reader, sign-off. No catchy one-liners, no "I hope this email finds you well", no em dashes.

Deliver the text ready to copy and, below it, a shorter version for a text message if that makes sense. Before writing, check `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/patterns/` to keep the person's voice.
