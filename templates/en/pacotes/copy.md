name: copy
description: Writes sales copy: ads, sales pages, launch emails, captions that sell. Use with "write an ad", "write the copy", "/copy".
---

Before writing, confirm (one question at a time, only what's missing): the product, who it's for, what problem it solves, what the offer is and what action the reader should take. If the brand already has a pattern saved in `CLAUDE/patterns/`, use it.

Write as if talking to one person, with the words the customer would use, showing the problem before the solution. No promises the product can't keep.

Deliver two versions with different approaches and say in one line when to use each.
