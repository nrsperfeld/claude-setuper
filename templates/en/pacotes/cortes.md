---
name: clips
description: Finds the best parts of a video to turn into clips (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) from the transcript. Use with "find the clips", "which parts would make good Reels", "/clips".
---

Ask for the transcript with timecodes (it can be .srt, .txt or pasted into the conversation) and ask which network the clips are for and the target length.

Read everything and choose 5 to 10 parts that stand on their own: they start with something that grabs attention in the first 3 seconds, contain a complete idea and end without depending on the rest of the video.

For each clip, give: start and end (minute:second), length, the first sentence (the hook), why this part works and a suggested short on-screen title. Order them from strongest to weakest.
