---
name: discovery
description: Records an insight or finding that came up during the work (CLAUDE/discoveries/). Use when something non-obvious appears, when the person says "that's interesting", "note this idea", "/discovery".
---

Save a new note in `$CLAUDE_SETUPER_VAULT/CLAUDE/discoveries/` named `<short summary of the idea>.md`.

Format (no frontmatter, no heading):
- First line: the idea, in one complete sentence that stands on its own.
- Then: where it came from and what it changes, if anything.
- Last line: `#claude #discovery`

One idea per note. If there's a related note, cite its name in double brackets, like this: [[note name]]. Confirm to the person in one line.
